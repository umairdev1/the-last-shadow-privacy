The Last Shadow

Privacy Policy · A game by SaleemKit · Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game The Last Shadow (package com.lastshadow.game) handles your information.

Summary

The Last Shadow does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The game works fully offline.

Information we collect

None. The game has no user accounts, does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and does not request the internet permission.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following only on your own device, using standard Android local storage:

  • your progress (unlocked and completed chapters, the last checkpoint, secrets found);
  • your settings (sound and music volume, control size and opacity, graphics quality).

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage in Android settings.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of the game contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking libraries. If a future version adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing exactly what data the advertising service uses.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

No information is shared with third parties. The game is distributed through Google Play; Google’s own privacy policy applies to your use of the Play Store.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
